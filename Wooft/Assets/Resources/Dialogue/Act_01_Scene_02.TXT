#INTRO
The hall receives you as though it has been waiting.
The courtiers stand in loose ranks, pale masks lifted at last from obedient faces. Some are set carefully aside.
Others are loosed to the floor, discarded with a faint, practiced relief. What is revealed is not relief.
The silken void whispers. Breath returns.
At the far end, the throne rests.
What sits there does not rise, yet nothing in the hall stands apart from it.
[CAMILLA]:  You, sir, should unmask.
[THE STRANGER]: Indeed?
[CASSILDA]: Indeed, it's time. We all have laid aside disguise but you.

#UNMASK
The court gasps as you raise your hands and mimic the tearing away of an unseen mask, casting it upward as the others once did.
Yet at once you feel the weight of something upon your brow, resting where no hand placed it—pressing softly against your temples.
A pale flicker skirts the edge of your sight.
...A mask now rests upon your face.
[THE KING]: The face departs. Yet the hollow it leaves is named—and it has learned to speak you.
[THE STRANGER]: Names are given to what may be possessed. I am not such a thing.
[THE KING]: Once you are named, are you not already claimed?
[THE STRANGER]: Does one ever truly know another? And if one could—would one endure it?
[THE KING]: No. Most are content with the mercy of ignorance, and call the mask the face.
[THE STRANGER]: All know your mask, my King.
[THE KING]: Indeed. Few have ever sought my face.
[THE STRANGER]: Then are you not the Stranger here?

#REFUSE
[THE STRANGER]: I wear no mask.
[CAMILLA]: (Terrified, aside to Cassilda) No mask? No mask!
Silence falls—heavy, sumptuous. The Stranger stands unveiled. The courtiers draw back, then compose themselves with practised elegance.
The silence lingers, indulgent, almost kind. Then it allows a voice to speak.
[THE KING]: You come unmasked. Do you imagine that makes you unseen?
[THE STRANGER]: I wished to arrive as I am.
[THE KING]: And are you so certain that you are unchanged? From a moment past? From yesterday?

#ALWAYSTHESAME
[THE STRANGER]: Yes. I know no other way of being.
[CAMILLA]: Then you are most deceived.
[THE STRANGER]: None know the Stranger. For once known, he ceases to be one.
[THE KING]: Just so. But I now know you.
[THE STRANGER]: You know my mask, my King, as I know yours. I have read the third act.
[CASSILDA]: There is no third act.
[THE KING]: Then why do you tremble as though it had only just begun?
YOU HAVE REACHED THE END OF THE DEMO.

#ALWAYSCHANGING
[THE STRANGER]: No. I am not the same. I have never remained.
[CAMILLA]: An honest face. How rare. Yet here you stand.
[THE STRANGER]: I remain a stranger to you, my King. That must suffice.
[THE KING]: As you must. For once you are known you would belong.
[THE STRANGER]: Belonging is not a mask I can wear, my King.
[THE KING]: Indeed.
YOU HAVE REACHED THE END OF THE DEMO.

#KINGUNLOVED
[THE KING]: All know of me. Where I walk it is never as an intruder ...but as someone who has always been expected.
[THE STRANGER]: Anticipation ends the moment one is known.
[THE KING]: Do you ever return to a mask once cast aside?
[THE STRANGER]: Never. Though I have been known to revisit a face.
[THE KING]: And what face do you expect to find hidden beneath MY mask?
[THE STRANGER]: My own. For if a King must remain a Stranger, then only The Stranger should be The King.
YOU HAVE REACHED THE END OF THE DEMO.

#KINGLOVED
[THE KING]: Ah. They say so. They say many things. They know the mask well.
[THE STRANGER]: Is knowledge love? Or merely familiarity? One cannot know a Stranger.
[THE KING]: My subjects love what I withhold from them—the veil beyond the world, the terror that the mask may slip.
[THE KING]: Were they to love me truly, they would beg me leave.
[THE STRANGER]: Yet they do not.
[THE KING]: And so I remain. And this they name love.
[THE STRANGER]: Would you beg ME leave, my King?
YOU HAVE REACHED THE END OF THE DEMO.
